FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Have another question? Feel free to include it in the consultation form.

Where does the treatment take place?+

Armamed is located in Ataşehir, Istanbul. This landing page is designed for people in the Netherlands who wish to request an online initial assessment.

What is needed for an initial assessment?+

Start with your name, email address and a brief explanation. For a more focused assessment, Armamed may then ask for photos of the front, top, sides and back.

What is FUE?+

With the FUE technique, hair follicles are extracted one by one from the donor area. Armamed describes this as one of its treatment options.

Is this medical advice?+

No. The information on this page is general and does not replace personal medical advice, diagnosis or treatment from a physician.
